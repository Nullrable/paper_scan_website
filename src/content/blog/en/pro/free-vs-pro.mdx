Paper Scanner is fully usable for free. Pro exists for the small set of users who genuinely need more — three specific improvements, bundled into one subscription.

## What Pro unlocks

**1. Unlimited OCR.** The free tier OCRs a generous daily number of pages. Pro removes the cap and lets you OCR an entire folder in one tap. Worth it if you regularly scan hundreds of pages a week.

**2. No banner ads.** The free tier shows a small banner at the top of the Documents list. Pro removes it. Worth it if you open the library dozens of times a day for work.

**3. Automatic cloud sync.** Free users trigger a manual backup whenever they want. Pro runs the sync in the background — the cloud mirror is always up to date. Worth it if you switch devices often or want a continuous off-site backup.

## When Pro is not for you

If you scan a few pages a week, organise them in folders, and export to PDF occasionally, the free tier is genuinely enough. We deliberately do not gate basic features behind Pro.

## How subscription works

Pro is sold through the App Store or Google Play — Paper Scanner never handles payment credentials directly. Subscriptions auto-renew until you cancel; cancellation is a one-tap operation in your phone's subscription settings. Refund policy follows the store's standard terms.

## What happens if you cancel

Your existing scans stay on the device. The previously uploaded cloud copies stay in your Drive or iCloud. You simply lose the three upgrades and return to the free tier experience. There is no grace period drama and no data deletion — Pro is a feature toggle, not a wall.
