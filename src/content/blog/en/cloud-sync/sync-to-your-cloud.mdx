Cloud sync in Paper Scanner does one thing: mirror your library to a cloud account you already own. Your phone stays the source of truth — the cloud is just a backup you control.

## A guardrail, not a limitation

The mirror is one-way by default — uploads only, no background downloads. This is intentional. If a teammate reorganises the shared Drive folder, or you sign in to a different device, those changes never overwrite what is on your phone.

In practice this means:

- You can rename, tag, and reorganise folders with zero risk of a sync undoing your work.
- A shared cloud folder with collaborators cannot accidentally clobber your latest edits.
- A device you sign out of cannot magically come back later filled with stale data.

## How to turn it on

Open **Settings → Cloud**, pick Google Drive or iCloud, sign in, and choose which folders to mirror. The first sync uploads whatever you have today; new scans follow automatically once that is set up.

You can switch providers at any time. Disabling cloud sync leaves your data exactly where you put it — nothing is deleted from the cloud by Paper Scanner. You decide when and whether to clean up the mirror.

## Restore is a one-tap action

If you switch phones, lose the device, or want to rebuild from scratch, **Settings → Cloud → Restore** brings everything back from the cloud mirror. It does not run in the background and it does not happen automatically — you always know when the library is being rebuilt, and you decide when to start.

## Free vs Pro

Free users trigger a manual backup any time they want — great if you only sync once a week or after big projects. Pro adds automatic background sync so the mirror is always up to date without you thinking about it. Same destination, same privacy — just less clicking.

## Where the bytes go

The mirror lives in **your** Google Drive or iCloud account. Paper Scanner does not run a relay server. The bytes travel directly between your phone and the storage you already own. No middleman, no copy on our infrastructure, no analytics tied to your documents.
