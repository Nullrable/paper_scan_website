OCR (optical character recognition) is what turns a photograph of paper into text you can select, copy, and search. In Paper Scanner it runs entirely on your device, so a scanned page becomes useful the moment it lands in your library.

## What OCR unlocks

Once a page has been OCR'd:

- You can long-press any word to copy it into another app.
- The search bar at the top of Documents looks inside every page, not just titles.
- A scanned receipt becomes quotable — paste the merchant name into an email without retyping.

## How to get the best results

OCR accuracy depends on three things, in order of importance:

1. **Lighting.** Even, indirect light beats a single bright overhead. Avoid shadows across the page.
2. **Focus.** Tap the centre of the page before capture if the auto-focus picked the wrong subject.
3. **Filter choice.** Black & white gives the cleanest text for printed forms; magic color helps when ink has faded.

## Free vs Pro

The free tier OCRs a generous number of pages per day — enough for everyday receipts, business cards, and the occasional book chapter. If you regularly OCR hundreds of pages, Pro removes the daily cap and lets you run OCR on the whole library in one go.

## Languages

The on-device engine supports Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and most European scripts out of the box. If a page has mixed scripts (for example, a menu with English and Japanese), OCR handles both in a single pass.
