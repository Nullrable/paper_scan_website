Almost every scanner app eventually tells you to stand up and step back. Paper Scanner does not — and once you get used to it, going back feels broken.

## Why the lens matters

The ultra-wide lens captures a much wider field of view than the default 1× camera. Hold the phone 30 cm above an A4 page and the entire page fills the viewfinder. For an A3 handout or a tabletop menu, you still do not need to step back.

## What you can capture in one shot

- A full A3 meeting handout, taken from a seated position
- A classroom whiteboard before the next group walks in
- A restaurant menu or magazine spread, even with thick margins
- Bound documents, page by page, without flattening the spine

Edge detection, perspective correction, and shadow removal all run the moment you tap capture, so the result is a clean, flat page — not a photograph of paper.

## When to switch to 1×

Ultra-wide is not always the right tool. Switch to the standard lens when:

- The page is small and dense (contracts, legal text, fine print) — corner sharpness matters more than field of view.
- The media is glossy (laminated IDs, magazine covers) — the wider aperture gathers more reflection at the edges.

## The hint you may see

If your hand jitters too much, the app shows a "hold steady" reminder. Take a breath, rest your elbows on the table, and try again. The shutter only fires when the frame is stable.
