Paper Scanner 1.0 is the first stable release of the app on the App Store and Google Play.

## Highlights

- Ultra-wide capture with auto edge detection and perspective correction
- Six GPU-powered filters rendered in real time on the device
- Local document library with folders, tags, grid and list views, and full-text search
- On-device OCR supporting Latin and CJK scripts
- Multi-page PDF export with optional password protection
- Optional one-way sync to your own Google Drive or iCloud
- Free tier with everything you need; Pro adds unlimited OCR, no ads, and automatic sync

## System requirements

- **iOS** 14 or later (iPhone 7 and newer recommended for ultra-wide)
- **Android** 8.0 (API 26) or later
- About 40 MB of storage for the app, plus whatever your scans add

## What is intentionally not in 1.0

- Shared libraries between multiple accounts (planned for a future release)
- Direct upload to OneDrive or Dropbox (you can always use the share sheet to send files there)
- A web companion view (your cloud mirror is already accessible in Drive or iCloud)

## How to send feedback

- **In-app**: Settings → Send Feedback
