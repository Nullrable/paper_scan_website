Paper Scanner's export is built for the everyday case: you have just finished a long meeting, you have 50 page scans on your phone, and you need to send them as one file to a colleague.

## The three-step flow

1. **Select** the scans in the Documents list (multi-select is built for this).
2. **Tap Share → PDF**. The export sheet opens.
3. **Choose your options** — merge into one PDF, optionally set an open password — then tap **Export**.

The result is a single compact PDF saved to your phone and offered through the standard share sheet. Send it via email, drop it into Drive, AirDrop to a Mac, print it, or save it for later.

## Owner vs user password

The export sheet has two independent password fields:

- **User password** — required to open the file at all. Use this for sensitive documents.
- **Owner password** — restricts editing and printing. Use this when you want recipients to view but not modify.

Leave both blank for a normal shareable PDF.

## When to split instead of merge

A single 200-page PDF is fine to email but slow to open on older devices. If your batch is bigger than that, consider splitting by section or by recipient. Paper Scanner exports the selection you choose — there is no forced merge.

## Performance notes

Merging and encryption both run locally, so a 50-page export typically finishes in a few seconds. Very large batches (300+ pages) may take 15-30 seconds; the progress is shown on the export sheet, so you can keep using the app in the meantime.
