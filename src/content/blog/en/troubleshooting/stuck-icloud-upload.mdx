One of the most common support tickets we receive: a user enables iCloud sync, the Documents status bar shows "uploading 12 of 50", and it stays there for five minutes. Ninety-nine percent of the time, the cause is one of four things below.

## 1. iCloud Drive is not actually on for Paper Scanner

There are two separate switches you need to verify:

- **iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Show all → Paper Scanner** must be toggled on.
- **iOS Settings → Paper Scanner → iCloud Drive** must also be toggled on.

If either is off, the app queues uploads but the system never accepts them.

## 2. iOS is throttling background sync

iOS actively throttles iCloud background activity in two situations:

- **Low Power Mode** is on — toggle it off in iOS Settings → Battery.
- **iPhone storage is nearly full** — iOS needs at least 500 MB free for background uploads. Free up space in iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage.

The UI will not say "storage full". It just shows "uploading" forever.

## 3. The network is fine but the queue is large

Uploads process sequentially. If you have hundreds of scans queued from a long offline stretch, expect the queue to take a while. Leave the app open in the background for 15-30 minutes before assuming something is wrong.

## 4. iCloud account itself is in a bad state

This is rare but real:

- iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → toggle iCloud Drive off, wait 30 seconds, toggle it back on.
- Sign out of iCloud in iOS Settings → your name → Sign Out, then sign back in. This refreshes the iCloud connection end to end.

## If you are still stuck

Use **Settings → Send Feedback** inside the app. The report carries the recent sync activity along with it, so we can see exactly what the app was doing when uploads stalled. Bug reports get a response within a few days.
